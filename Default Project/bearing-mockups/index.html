Three workflows, one engine

The goal here is not visual polish — it is to feel out how you want to work, so the build has a concrete target. All three run on the same deterministic engine and the same brief; they differ only in how you reach it and how much the advisor does in front of you.

Terminal-first

On-demand, keyboard-driven, scriptable. You pull context with a command and pipe it anywhere. A shell hook can drop a one-liner when you open a terminal.

How to read these. They are deliberately different paradigms, not three screens of one app. Ask yourself:
· Do I want context pulled on demand, or pushed at me?
· Do I think better in a terminal or in a visual board?
· How much should the advisor do in front of me versus stay in the background?
Answering those picks the surface — and the surface is the only thing that changes; the engine underneath is the same.
